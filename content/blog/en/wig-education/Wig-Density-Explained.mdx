## What Does Wig Density Actually Mean?

Wig density refers to how much hair is added to the wig cap.

It affects:
- Volume
- Weight
- Movement
- Realism
- Comfort

The most common options are:
- 130%
- 150%
- 180%

Choosing the wrong one can make your wig look too flat — or too bulky.

Let’s break them down clearly.

---

# 130% Density – Light & Natural

### How It Looks

- Close to natural hair thickness  
- Soft movement  
- Lightweight appearance  

This is the most realistic option for everyday wear.

### Best For

✔ Daily use  
✔ Professional settings  
✔ First-time wig wearers  
✔ Petite face shapes  

### Pros

- Breathable  
- Easy to blend  
- Very natural in daylight  

### Cons

- May feel “too thin” if you're used to very full hair  
- Less dramatic styling volume  

If your goal is “undetectable,” 130% wins.

---

# 150% Density – Balanced & Versatile

### How It Looks

- Medium fullness  
- Noticeable but not heavy  
- Holds curls well  

This is the most popular density choice.

### Best For

✔ Soft glam looks  
✔ Layered cuts  
✔ Shoulder to long length styles  
✔ People wanting both realism and fullness  

### Pros

- Good styling flexibility  
- Photographs well  
- Still wearable daily  

### Cons

- Slightly heavier than 130%  
- Needs crown control for realism  

150% is the safe middle ground.

---

# 180% Density – Full & Dramatic

### How It Looks

- Very thick  
- High volume  
- Glamorous appearance  

It creates impact instantly.

### Best For

✔ Events  
✔ Very long lengths (24"+)  
✔ Bold styling  
✔ Big curls  

### Pros

- Holds dramatic styles  
- Looks luxurious in photos  

### Cons

- Can look bulky at the roots  
- Harder to blend lace  
- Heavier on the scalp  

For everyday wear, it can feel excessive.

---

## How Density Affects Realism

More density doesn’t mean more natural.

In fact:

Lower density often:
- Blends lace better  
- Creates softer hairlines  
- Looks more expensive  

High density:
- Increases bulk  
- Emphasizes wig structure  

Balance is key.

---

## How to Choose the Right Density

Ask yourself:

1. Do I want ultra-natural or glamorous?
2. Will I wear this daily?
3. Is my face shape small, round, or long?
4. Am I sensitive to weight?

Quick guide:

- Natural everyday → 130%
- Balanced daily glam → 150%
- Event / bold styling → 180%

---

## Face Shape & Density

Round face:
Avoid too much side volume.

Long face:
Medium density adds balance.

Small frame:
Heavy density may overwhelm.

Bone structure matters more than trend.

---

## Final Verdict

If you’re unsure — choose 150%.

If realism is your priority — choose 130%.

If drama is your goal — choose 180%.

The best density is the one that matches your lifestyle, not just your inspiration photo.
