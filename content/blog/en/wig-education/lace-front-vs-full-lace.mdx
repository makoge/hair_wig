## Quick answer
Lace front wigs look natural at the hairline. Full lace offers the most styling freedom (updos, parting anywhere).

## Lace front wigs
- Best for: beginners, daily wear, budget
- Strength: natural hairline
- Limitation: less freedom for high ponytails

## Full lace wigs
- Best for: updos, frequent styling changes
- Strength: part anywhere + maximum realism
- Tradeoff: cost + maintenance

## What to choose
If you want the easiest “natural look” start: lace front.  
If you want updos and versatility: full lace.
