## What matters most
Comfort first: breathable cap, soft materials, no pressure points.

## Best options
- Glueless lace wigs for daily wear
- Lightweight density for comfort
- Adjustable straps + secure comb placement

## Sensitive scalp tips
Patch test everything. Prioritize gentle cleansers and low-tack products.
