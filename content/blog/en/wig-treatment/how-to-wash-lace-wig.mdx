## Before you wash
Detangle from ends to roots. Never scrub lace.

## Washing steps
1. Lukewarm water
2. Sulfate-free shampoo (light)
3. Condition mids-to-ends
4. Rinse gently
5. Pat dry, air dry on a stand

## Make it last longer
Wash less, style smarter, store properly.
