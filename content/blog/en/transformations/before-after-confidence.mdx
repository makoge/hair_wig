## The 5-minute realism checklist
- Natural part width
- Baby hairs (minimal)
- Lace tint match
- Flat top (no bumps)
- Flyaway control

## The #1 mistake
Over-plucking or heavy baby hairs. Less is more for “real scalp” realism.
