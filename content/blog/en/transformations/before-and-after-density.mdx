## Why Wig Density Matters More Than You Think

Most people focus on length and color when buying a wig.

But density is what truly determines whether your wig looks:
- Natural
- Glamorous
- Overwhelming
- Or completely fake

The difference between 130% and 180% density can completely transform your face.

Let’s break down what really changes — before and after.

---

## What Is Wig Density?

Wig density refers to how much hair is sewn into the cap.

Common options:
- 130% (light / natural)
- 150% (medium / balanced)
- 180% (full / dramatic)

More density = more volume.

But more volume doesn’t always mean better.

---

# BEFORE & AFTER: 130% Density

## Before

If you’re used to heavy, thick wigs, 130% may look “too thin” at first glance.

But that perception changes fast.

## After

130% density:
- Mimics natural hair thickness
- Moves freely
- Looks realistic in daylight
- Feels lightweight and breathable

Best for:
- Everyday wear
- Professional settings
- First-time wig users
- Petite frames

If your goal is “Is that your real hair?” — this wins.

---

# BEFORE & AFTER: 150% Density

## Before

This is the most popular choice — and for good reason.

Balanced, not extreme.

## After

150% density:
- Adds soft fullness
- Works beautifully with layers
- Holds curls better
- Still looks natural if styled correctly

Best for:
- Soft glam
- Waves
- Face-framing cuts
- Medium-length styles

It’s the safest option for most people.

---

# BEFORE & AFTER: 180% Density

## Before

Looks amazing in photos.

Feels luxurious instantly.

## After

In real life, 180% density:
- Can appear bulky at the roots
- Makes lace harder to blend
- May overwhelm smaller face shapes

Best for:
- Events
- Long lengths (24"+)
- Dramatic styling
- High-glam looks

Not always ideal for daily wear.

---

## How Density Changes Face Shape

Density impacts visual balance.

Round face:
- Too much side volume widens the face
- 130–150% works best

Long face:
- Avoid flat styles
- Medium density adds balance

Small frame:
- Heavy density can overpower

Your bone structure should guide your density choice.

---

## The Real Before & After Effect

Lower density often creates:
- Softer hairlines
- Easier lace blending
- More natural scalp illusion
- More expensive-looking finish

Ironically, less hair often looks more luxurious.

---

## Quick Density Decision Guide

Choose 130% if:
✔ You want ultra natural  
✔ You wear wigs daily  
✔ Comfort matters most  

Choose 150% if:
✔ You want balanced fullness  
✔ You love soft glam  

Choose 180% if:
✔ You want drama  
✔ You style heavily  
✔ You don’t mind extra maintenance  

---

## Final Truth

If your wig looks fake, density is often the hidden reason.

Before buying a new wig, reconsider the volume.

Sometimes the biggest transformation comes from choosing less.
